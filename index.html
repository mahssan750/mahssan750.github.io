Flagship project

An end-to-end machine learning system, from raw transactions to a ranked call list.

Customer churn prediction

Identify at-risk customers before they leave

Every online retailer faces the same silent threat: customers who stop buying without warning. Retaining one costs 5–7× less than acquiring a new one — so the earlier you spot a churner, the cheaper the save. This system reads each customer's full transaction history and tells the retention team exactly who to call, who to email, and who to leave alone.

How it works
  • Engineer behavioural features from real e-commerce transactions — recency, frequency, monetary value and trend signals per customer.
  • Train & compare 3 models — Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost, tuned with Optuna hyperparameter search.
  • Score every customer 0–100% and assign a risk tier, so retention effort goes where it pays off.
  • Serve in production — FastAPI endpoint, scheduled daily retraining, a file-scoring client and a pytest suite.
